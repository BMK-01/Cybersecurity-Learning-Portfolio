# Defensive Security Basics

## Overview
Defensive security focuses on preventing, detecting, and responding to cyber attacks. 
It involves implementing reactive and proactive measures to protect an organization's network, data, and users from malicious adversaries.

## Security Relevance
Defensive security is the backbone of protecting digital infrastructure because it actively mitigates risks and neutralizes ongoing threats. 
Security teams rely on these practices to monitor suspicious network activities, investigate successful breaches, and analyze malicious software to prevent future compromises.

## What I Learned (Learning Objectives)
* **Security Operations Center (SOC):** Discovered that SOC analysts act as the front line of defense by continuously monitoring networks for anomalies and triaging security alerts.
* **Threat Intelligence:** Learned how threat intelligence involves gathering and analyzing data about active threat actors, their tactics, and known malicious infrastructure to strengthen defenses proactively.
* **Digital Forensics and Incident Response (DFIR):** Explored how DFIR teams investigate compromised systems to determine the root cause of a breach and safely recover the affected network.
* **Malware Analysis:** Understood the process of reverse engineering malicious software to determine its capabilities and extract indicators of compromise.

## What I Practiced (Hands-on labs)
* Investigated a simulated SOC dashboard alert flagging a suspicious login attempt on a target machine.
* Copied the attacker's source IP address and analyzed it using a built-in threat intelligence reputation checker.
* Confirmed the IP address was malicious and escalated the response by blocking the IP on the simulated firewall.
* Retrieved the final hidden flag upon successfully neutralizing the threat.
